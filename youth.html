About Us

Lebanese Forces USA Young Professionals is the official youth organization of the Lebanese Forces in the United States, dedicated to educating and engaging Lebanese youth around the principles of sovereignty, democracy, and national responsibility while preparing future leaders for service to Lebanon and the Lebanese Forces. The Lebanese Forces USA Young Professionals will help you in your journey to becoming a real member of the Lebanese Forces.
